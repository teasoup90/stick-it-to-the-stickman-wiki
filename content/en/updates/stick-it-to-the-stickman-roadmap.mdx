The Stick It to the Stickman roadmap is best answered honestly: there is no formal roadmap document in the saved sources. What exists instead is a chain of official announcements that function as one, from the early access plan through 1.0 and into the coming-weeks list.

That chain is actually more useful than a roadmap, because every item on it is either shipped or explicitly abandoned, and the team's own words explain which is which.

This Stick It to the Stickman roadmap guide reconstructs that chain from the saved announcements and marks each item as delivered, planned, or cancelled.

The summary: everything teased before 1.0 either shipped at launch or was openly abandoned, and the only active plan is the coming-weeks polish list.

That summary is the whole page in one sentence, and the sections below expand each piece with the team's own words from the saved announcements.

## The Roadmap That Was: Early Access

The early access period ran from August 18, 2025 to September 23, 2026, and its roadmap lived in the regular updates the team posted. The saved record shows new archetypes, weapons, passives, bosses, and level segments arriving through that window.

The August 14 pre-launch post is the best single summary: two new archetypes, the Ninja and the Negotiator, new secondary weapons for all archetypes, and a long fix list.

## The Roadmap That Was: 1.0

The 1.0 plan was announced in the same August post: launch on September 23, a price move to $12, and new game modes. The launch post confirms the delivery: the ultimate boss fight at the bottom of a billionaire's bunker and the Neurolink gamemode.

So the 1.0 roadmap, as far as the saved record shows, was delivered on schedule and in substance.

The price note adds a business dimension to the roadmap story: the team announced the $12 launch price weeks ahead, which let players plan purchases around the schedule.
