## What the Team Left Unfinished

The launch post is candid about scope: game modes the team teased, including the Marketing Department, the Robot Takeover of Fulfilment, the Science Center, and Employee Evaluation Trials, would have taken another year or more to finish, and it was time for the team to move on.

That means those modes are not coming in the post-launch plans listed above, which are optimisation and polish rather than new mode development.

The launch post's candidness here is unusual and useful: instead of letting players hope for the teased modes, the team closed that door in the same post that launched 1.0.
