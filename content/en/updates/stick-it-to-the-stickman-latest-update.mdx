The newest Stick It to the Stickman update is the **13 October 2026** patch, posted on the [Steam news page](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2085540) under the title "Market Fixing and No Secrets." It is a maintenance patch — bug fixes, localisation work, UI cleanup and a new escape hatch for stuck runs — rather than a content drop. The game's 1.0 release arrived on 23 September 2026, and as of 2026-10-18 the Steam news page does not list a date for the next patch or a published roadmap.

## What the 13 October 2026 patch changed

The notes are written in the team's usual corporate-satire voice, complete with a running "No New Content Policy" joke, but the actual changelog is housekeeping. The headline items:

- **An "Unstuck" button in every game mode.** This is the most broadly useful addition, since the patch is largely about runs that could trap players.
- **Softlock fixes** in Foreign Manufacturing on ultrawide monitors, in Fulfillment, and when collecting a promotion during the final boss fight. Killing all Neurolink Volunteers no longer locks that quest.
- **Combat and mode corrections:** Feral Bite no longer launches the player out of the map in Neurolink, Bo-Nado no longer breaks the Perfections combo style, summons in the final boss fight now die when the boss does, and the Shareholder Dance scene works with two players.
- **Behaviour tweaks:** the Awesome Mustache now affects punch damage, Dragon Flex effects disappear properly, and the Bunker Armoury now requires the bunker to be purchased.
- **Localisation and UI:** Turkish special characters display correctly, and the notes mention "many fixes to UI." A Secret Quest is listed twice as working better, which is part of the joke.

No new archetypes, modes or levels were added in this patch.

## 1.0: what shipped on 23 September 2026

The 1.0 update added the **Neurolink** game mode and the game's final boss fight, set at the bottom of a billionaire's bunker. The launch post also confirmed that several previously teased modes would not be finished: the Marketing Department, the Robot Takeover of Fulfilment, the Science Center and Employee Evaluation Trials. The team said completing them would have taken another year or more.

## Recent updates before 1.0

- **14 August 2026** — Foreign Manufacturing expanded with the Ninja and Negotiator archetypes, new secondary weapons available to all archetypes, and extra passives, bosses and level segments.
- **4 May 2026** — Foreign Manufacturing released as a new mode, unlocked through the Stockmarket, with five new archetypes.
- **4 February 2026** — Company History Month, a story mode about the Founder's rise.
- **4 December 2025** — the Level Builder arrived, with custom enemies, props and Steam Workshop sharing.

## Is another update coming?

The 1.0 announcement is the closest thing to a plan: it said to expect a big optimisation pass, bug fixes, Steam Trading Cards and other community items, and minor polish "in the coming weeks." No dates were attached to any of those. As of 2026-10-18, the Steam news page does not list a roadmap or a scheduled date for a future update.

An earlier post from 7 August 2026 explained the shape of things: the team had gone from eight developers to four, and unfinished modes such as Rise of the Machines and Marketing Department were cut so the game could reach 1.0. That makes further large content updates unlikely, but it is an inference from the posted plan rather than a formal end-of-support statement.

## If a run gets stuck

The Unstuck button added in the 13 October 2026 patch is the obvious first thing to try; the notes don't spell out where it sits in each mode. If a softlock survives that, verifying the game files through Steam is a standard next step, and the Steam discussions and the game's Discord — both linked from the store page — are where the team has been collecting bug reports. Those are general suggestions, not features documented in the patch notes.
