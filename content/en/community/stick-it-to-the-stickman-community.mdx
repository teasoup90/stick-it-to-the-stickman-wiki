If you want to talk about *Stick It to the Stickman* with other players, the Steam Community Hub is the place that is always open and always public, and the game's Discord is the place Free Lives actually points people toward. A Reddit button sits in the same row of links on the Steam store page, but that page does not name a specific subreddit, so treat Reddit as somewhere to search rather than a known destination.

## The Steam Community Hub

The [discussion board](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2085540/discussions/) is split into General Discussions, Bug Reports & Technical Issues, Workshop Discussions, Trading, and Events & Announcements. It is the lowest-friction option: no invite needed, and threads are readable without an account, though posting requires signing in.

Two pinned threads are worth knowing about before you start a new topic. One is "How to report bugs!", which sets out what the moderators want in a bug post. The other is a guide to the Level Builder's edit mode written by someone at Free Lives, which is the closest thing to developer documentation for that tool. If you are stuck on an unlock, a softlock, or a boss, searching General Discussions first will usually turn up an existing thread — the board is small enough that most questions have been asked before.

A few conventions that keep the board useful:

- Put crashes, performance complaints and progression bugs in Bug Reports & Technical Issues rather than General Discussions, and follow the pinned format so the report is actionable.
- Keep spoilers out of thread titles. Secret archetypes, secret quests and the final boss are all things people are still discovering, and the patch notes themselves redact some of them.
- Say which mode you were playing. A lot of confusion comes from advice about one mode being applied to another.

## The Discord

The Steam store page carries a Discord link in its links section, and that is the stable way in — no invite code is printed here because the store page link is the one that stays current. Free Lives' Steam announcements treat it as the main community channel: the 1.0 launch post thanks community playtesters and people who "posted a meme in the Discord", the Foreign Manufacturing update asks players to send status reports "on our Discord", and the December 2025 Level Builder competition took entries through Discord. If you want to be near the developers rather than just other players, that is where they have said they are.

Expect it to be the liveliest place right after a patch, and the best place to flag something that looks like a genuine bug rather than a skill issue.

## Reddit and the other links

The Steam store page's links row also includes Reddit alongside X, YouTube, Twitch, Instagram, TikTok, Bluesky, Facebook and LinkedIn. As of 2026-10-18, the Steam store page does not name the subreddit behind that button, and Free Lives' Steam announcements direct players to Discord rather than to a subreddit, so searching Reddit for the game's name is more reliable than guessing a URL. The YouTube and Twitch links are useful if you would rather watch runs than read about them.

## Playing together and sharing levels

The Steam store page lists single-player, shared/split screen co-op, Remote Play Together, Steam Workshop support and an included level editor. It does not advertise matchmaking, and the discussion board has no dedicated looking-for-group section, so asking in General Discussions or on Discord is the practical route to finding someone for a Remote Play Together session.

For custom content, the Workshop and its discussion subforum are where levels and imported props circulate. The store page lists Steam Workshop as a feature, and the developer's Level Builder announcement describes uploading and downloading levels and `.glb` props there.

## A note on versions

Discussion threads rarely state a build number, and much of the advice in older posts predates the 1.0 release on 23 September 2026. If a thread describes an unlock method or a menu that no longer matches what you see, check the date on the post and the [patch notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2085540) before assuming you are doing something wrong.
