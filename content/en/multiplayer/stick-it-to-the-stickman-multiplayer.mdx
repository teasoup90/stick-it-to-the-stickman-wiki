## How the Co-Op Works

The Co-Optimus profile gives the exact trigger: after the prologue, a second player can jump in and help fight all your coworkers with his own stick man. That single sentence answers when, how, and with what.

The drop-in nature matters: the second player joins an existing run rather than starting a separate save, and they get their own stickman rather than controlling a shared one.

That also means the first player remains the host of the run, and the co-op session lives inside whatever run was already in progress when the second stickman appeared.

## The Co-Op Experience in Practice

The co-op experience, in Co-Optimus's own words, is fighting all your coworkers with a second stickman at your side. The game's physics brawler combat means the second player is not a sidekick system but a full second fighter.

That has a real consequence: the office chaos scales with the players. Community reports describe co-op runs as messier and funnier than solo runs, with two physics bodies colliding into the same fight.

Weapons, moves, and the climb all stay shared, so the second player experiences the same campaign rather than a truncated guest mode.

The saved sources also describe the game's own marketing line in the same breath as the co-op: the brawler fantasy is about firing coworkers and climbing the ladder, and the second stickman is part of that fantasy rather than an afterthought bolted on later.
