## What Remote Play Is in This Game

Remote Play in the game means Steam's Remote Play Together: the host runs the game, and the guest streams it, joining the co-op mode after the prologue like a local second player.

The Steam listing names Remote Play Together among the co-op options, which makes remote play an official feature rather than a workaround.

The experience is therefore local coop with a network layer: same drop-in trigger, same second stickman, different room.
