## What Local Coop Actually Means Here

Local coop in this game means two players on one machine, each with their own stickman, fighting the same campaign together. The Co-Optimus profile states that after the prologue, a second player can jump in and help fight all your coworkers with his own stick man.

That phrasing settles the two big questions: when (after the prologue) and what (a full second fighter, not a tag-along helper).

The same-machine part is implied by the couch co-op framing in the game description, which is what separates local coop from the Remote Play Together option.

## How Local Coop Changes the Game

Two stickmen change the physics as much as the tactics. The game's brawler combat is built around collisions, launches, and weapon swings, and a second body in the fight multiplies the chaos on every screen.

Community reports describe local sessions as louder and looser than solo runs, with the two players splitting the room or ganging up on bosses as the situation demands.

The campaign itself does not change: same rooms, same climb, same unlocks, just executed by a pair instead of a loner.

That constancy is worth stating because couch co-op games often compromise the solo experience, and nothing in the saved sources suggests this one does. The solo climb and the duo climb are the same climb with a different head count.
