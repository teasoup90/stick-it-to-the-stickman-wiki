## FAQ

### How many characters are in Stick It to the Stickman?

The saved sources do not state one total number. They document the standard workers, several secret characters, and the resource-gated unlocks, with the wiki Characters page as the living roster. Treat it as a living list that grows with patches.

### Which character should I unlock first?

Community guides point to the Vampire first among the secrets, because his Blood Donation loop also feeds the Necromancer's 50 Blood cost later. His route is the cheapest, it feeds the next unlock, and the donation loop pays for both characters over time.

### Are the secret characters worth the grind?

Community reports say yes. Each secret plays differently, and the guides describe them as the content that keeps the game fresh after the main climb. Each one is a project, not a pickup, and the payoff is a new playstyle, with the quests themselves being part of the fun.
