Blood comes from the Fulfillment Center's donation system, so the real grind is the same one the Vampire uses. Players in the thread describe saving Blood past the Vampire threshold specifically to afford the Necromancer's 50.

## The Navigation Bug

The thread's twist arrives in the replies: one player with 55 Blood reports the game will not let them navigate to unlock the Necromancer, with the selector jumping from the Vampire to the cowboy and skipping the Necromancer entirely. Others reply that they have the same issue, which makes it a pattern rather than one person's setup.

The fix that worked for at least one player: unlocking the other characters first. One player reports that unlocking the other characters like the Vampire fixed the navigation issue, and the game then allowed them to move up and select the Necromancer. Community replies treat it as a workaround, not an official fix.

The pattern's consistency suggests the selector's navigation state depends on which characters are already unlocked, which is exactly what the workaround implies. A Stick It to the Stickman Necromancer run hitting this bug should stop at the workaround before farming more, because extra Blood does not change the selector state.

## FAQ

### How do I get the Necromancer in Stick It to the Stickman?

Reach the employee evaluation center with 50 Blood from the Fulfillment Center, then select him on the unlock screen. He sits in the same upgrade path as the cowboy, and the full unlock is reachable on the screen where the cowboy unlocks.

### Why can't I select the Necromancer?

This is the thread's known navigation bug: the selector jumps from the Vampire to the cowboy and skips him. Unlocking the other characters like the Vampire fixed it for at least one player, and several replies in the Stick It to the Stickman Necromancer thread report the same stuck state.

### How much Blood does the Necromancer need?

Fifty Blood, saved from the Fulfillment Center donation loop, which is the same source the Vampire unlock uses for its 20. It is the steepest character cost the thread documents, and donation runs are the only named source in the thread.

### Where is the Stick It to the Stickman Necromancer unlock screen?

In the employee evaluation center, on the same path as the cowboy. If it will not let you select him, try unlocking the other characters first, then return. The workaround replies are the thread's top-rated help, and the link at the end of this page opens the full exchange.
