## How to Get the Vampire

You unlock the Vampire by donating blood: first unlock Blood Donation in the Fulfilment Centre, then collect 20 Blood during runs. That is the whole requirement, and the source walkthrough states it plainly before adding the step-by-step route below.

The Fulfilment Centre step matters most. Inside the centre you will find the Blood Donation upgrade, and this must be purchased before you can collect any Blood. Community threads confirm the same point from the player side: no upgrade, no drops.

The source walkthrough spells out the step order without padding: first reach the Fulfilment Centre, where Blood Donation waits inside, then play the Day Shift in the Amazon Centre, where Blood appears as a unique currency. Collect 20 of these and the Vampire unlocks for play, and if you miss Blood on one run it carries into the next.

## Where to Farm Blood

The source describes the Day Shift in the Amazon Centre as the place where Blood drops most reliably. Once Blood Donation is unlocked, head into the Amazon Centre and play through the Day Shift level, because that is where Blood appears as a unique currency during the run.

Blood carries over between runs, so any Blood you miss on one run is not lost. If a run ends short of the total, the counter keeps your progress, and the community habit is simply to keep grinding Day Shift until the 20 Blood total is reached.

Day Shift's drop pattern is also why the source says 'most reliably' rather than 'only': Blood can appear elsewhere, but Day Shift is the consistent choice, and consistency is what a grind needs. Players who ask for a Stick It to the Stickman vampire farming shortcut usually get the same answer from the community: there is no shortcut, just the Day Shift loop, and the loop is short enough that twenty Blood arrives faster than most other character grinds.

## Why the Vampire Needs Blood

Unlike most characters, the Vampire is locked behind its own resource system. Blood acts as his unique requirement, which sets him apart from standard employees or fighters, and the source frames it as a deliberate gate that makes him a later unlock.

The design point is progression: you must move through the Fulfilment Centre systems first, which guarantees you have seen the mid game before the Vampire joins you. Community reports read the same design in the other resource-gated characters, like the Necromancer's Blood cost, and it is the one detail every Stick It to the Stickman vampire guide repeats first.

That ordering also explains a common confusion: players who farm Blood before buying Blood Donation see zero drops and assume the Vampire is bugged. Community threads consistently point them back to the upgrade screen, not the bug forum.

## FAQ

### How do I unlock the Vampire in Stick It to the Stickman?

Unlock Blood Donation in the Fulfilment Centre, then collect 20 Blood during runs. The counter carries over, so every Day Shift run moves you closer. Buy the upgrade, run Day Shift, repeat, and there is no hidden trigger to find.

### How much Blood does the Vampire need?

You need 20 Blood to unlock the Vampire, and the total carries over between runs until you reach it. The source states the number plainly, so plan your spending around it and keep the counter in mind before spending Blood elsewhere.

### Where is the best Stick It to the Stickman Vampire farming spot?

The source names the Amazon Centre Day Shift as the most reliable drop location, after Blood Donation is purchased in the Fulfilment Centre. The Fulfilment Centre upgrade must come first, or no Blood drops at all.

### Does Blood carry over between runs?

Yes. Any Blood collected saves across multiple runs until you reach the 20 Blood total, so failed runs still count toward the unlock, which is what makes the Stick It to the Stickman vampire grind forgiving.
