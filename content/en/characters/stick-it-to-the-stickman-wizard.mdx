## What the After Party Page Documents

After Party is a passive exclusive to the Student. The page is short, and its effect line is the whole mechanic: when enemies come into contact with the Student, they barf, trip, and take 1 damage. No unlock route, no cost, just the passive and its owner, which is why the page reads more like a card description than a guide.

The contact-triggered effect reads like a defensive aura: anything that touches the Student pays a small price and gets disrupted. Community readers describe it as a crowd-breaker, useful when enemies swarm in tight rooms, and the 1 damage looks small until it interrupts an attack that would have connected, which is the value most newcomers underestimate.

For a Stick It to the Stickman wizard search, the Student is the character to learn about first, because the passive is exclusive to them and the wiki keeps its magic-adjacent content under that entry. The barf, trip, and damage effect is the only confirmed power, and it is tied to contact, not to any spell you can cast at range.
