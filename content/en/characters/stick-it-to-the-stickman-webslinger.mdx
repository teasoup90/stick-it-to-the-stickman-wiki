The Stick It to the Stickman Webslinger unlock route, as written in the saved John-DVD community guide, starts by opening presents around the city until a camera drops, then taking photos of three webs, then picking up a lasso and dragging three marked criminals to the police station door. The guide was posted on September 11, 2025 and updated on September 26, 2025; this page has not independently checked the route on a newer game build. One Steam bug report from August 12 says that completing the game with the Webslinger does not tag it as completed in the Warehouse, and the developer or moderator reply only said, “Noted - thank you.”

## The Community Guide Behind the Webslinger Route

The main source for the unlock steps is a Steam Community guide by John-DVD titled “Fulfillment center secret character guide [spoiler free].” The guide title uses the phrase “fulfillment center secret character,” while the separate Steam bug report uses the name “Webslinger.” This page uses Webslinger because that is the name used in the player report and in the question this page answers.

The guide was posted on September 11, 2025 and updated on September 26, 2025. For context, the official [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/) lists the Early Access release date as August 18, 2025 and the full release date as September 23, 2026. In other words, the guide was written during the pre-launch period and has not been independently checked on a newer game build.

John-DVD also wrote a note explaining that both instances of “fulfillment center secret character” used to be “new secret character” but they changed it because “its been a while.” That detail helps explain why older references may use different wording. The guide index lists the sections as Overview, STEP 1. getting a camera, STEP 2. taking photos, STEP 3. arresting the criminals, Note., and Comments. That same order is used here.

## Step-by-Step Webslinger Unlock Steps

The guide breaks the route into three named steps plus a final unlock line. The steps below repeat only what the guide states.

**Step 1: Get a camera from city presents.** The guide says to “go around the city opening presents until you get a camera.” It also warns that this “can take a while sometimes,” so the camera is not tied to a single stated present location. The guide does not say how many presents to open. It only states that the camera drops after opening presents.

**Step 2: Photograph all webs around the map.** You need to use the camera on three webs. The guide gives these positions:

| Web | Location | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Up and left of the Fulfillment Center | Use the camera to take a picture |
| 2 | Far left of the Finance Tower | Use the camera to take a picture |
| 3 | Down and right of the Evaluation Center | Use the camera to take a picture |

The guide adds that “taking the photos can be buggy sometimes.” It does not provide a workaround for that photo bug. The guide also does not say what to do if the phone icon does not appear. The only warning is that taking the photos can be buggy sometimes.

The guide’s comments section includes a few extra player notes about the camera search. One player wrote “camera near corner,” while another wrote “but i didn't find the camera.” The guide itself does not list a fixed camera spawn, only that opening presents can take a while.

**Step 3: Wait for the phone icon and pick up the lasso.** If the photos were taken correctly, “a phone icon will appear.” After that, “now a lasso will appear pick it up.” The guide uses clipped wording, but the order is phone icon first, then lasso. The guide does not state exactly where the lasso appears, only that it appears after the phone icon.

**Step 4: Bring three marked criminals to the police station.** The guide says, “now the game will mark 3 criminals use the lasso bring them to the door of the police station.” After that comes the line, “now you should unlock the new character.”

The final destination is therefore the police station door. The Fulfillment Center appears earlier as a photo location, but the final action does not happen there.

If you are following the route in real time, use this checklist:

- [ ] Open city presents until a camera drops
- [ ] Photograph the web up and left of the Fulfillment Center
- [ ] Photograph the web far left of the Finance Tower
- [ ] Photograph the web down and right of the Evaluation Center
- [ ] Wait for the phone icon to appear
- [ ] Pick up the lasso
- [ ] Drag the three marked criminals to the police station door

## FAQ

### How do you start the Stick It to the Stickman Webslinger unlock route?

Open presents around the city until a camera drops. That is the first step in the John-DVD community guide.

### Where does the Webslinger route end?

The route ends at the door of the police station after you use the lasso to bring three marked criminals there. The Fulfillment Center is only a web photo location, not the endpoint.

### What items are needed for the unlock?

The guide lists a camera for the three web photos and a lasso that appears after a phone icon. No other items are listed in the guide.

### Is the Warehouse completion marker bug fixed?

The saved bug report does not say it is fixed. The only developer or moderator reply is “Noted - thank you.”
