## The Community Unlock Route

The community's secret character guide describes the unlock as a photography quest. Find a camera, take pictures of spider webs all around the map, pick up the phone, catch all the criminals, and go to the Fulfillment Center. That is the full step order the guide gives, and it ends where most secret quests end: the Fulfillment Center.

Community reports describe the web photography as the slow part, because the webs are spread wide and some are easy to walk past. The criminal-catching step is the combat part, and players recommend doing it before the Fulfillment Center trip. The guide lists the steps in plain order and does not hide locations, but it does warn readers that the quest is buggy, so patience beats speed, and the whole route plays like a scavenger hunt with a fight at the end.

One more community note for a Stick It to the Stickman webslinger attempt: complete the criminal-catching step in the same run as the photography if possible, because reports describe the quest state as fragile across separate runs.

## The Warehouse Completion Bug

Completing the game with the Webslinger does not tag it as completed in the Warehouse, according to the saved bug report. The report was posted to the game's Steam discussions, and the poster framed it as a clear miss: the run finishes, but the completion flag stays off.

For completionists this matters, because the Warehouse tracking is how the game records which characters have cleared the game. A Webslinger clear that does not register means re-running the character later or waiting for a fix.

The bug's scope matters for planning a Stick It to the Stickman webslinger clear: if only the Warehouse tag is broken, your unlock and your win still happened, and only the completion record is wrong. Community reports read it that way, which is why the fix is a re-clear rather than a full re-unlock.

## Tracking Your Completion

If your Warehouse shows a character as uncleared after a win, the community advice is to re-run the final stretch after the next patch and watch the tracking screen before quitting. Some reports describe the tag appearing on a second clear even without a patch. Before a Stick It to the Stickman webslinger run, note your current Warehouse state, and after the clear, check it immediately, because the tag either sticks or it does not, and the difference tells you whether to re-run now or wait for a patch.

Community reports also note that quitting through unusual menus right after a clear seems to correlate with lost tags, so let the run end naturally.

The [saved Webslinger bug thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2085540/discussions/0/589560695544144030/) remains the place to check for the current status, and new reports keep landing there when patches shake the tracking system.
