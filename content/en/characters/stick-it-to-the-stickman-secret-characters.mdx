## Batman and Saitama Unlock Steps

These two unlocks are the most frequently repeated in search results for Stick It to the Stickman secret characters, so they are a good starting point.

### Batman / Night Shift

The Batman unlock starts in the city map, but it may also depend on the cinema or Night Shift area.

- Destroy every red CEO cutout around the map. Cchhey wrote, "Destroy Every red cutout around the map."
- After the cutouts are destroyed, follow the marker to the phone, pick it up, and defeat the CEOs the game marks.
- Cchhey's wanted-level tip: "after destroying a CEO, you get a 5 star wanted level. To make it easier, after every CEO, go through the car wash to reset your wanted level."
- YOKO added that Batman "just joins the night shift I think you also need to unlock the cinema."

The [Dark Knight guide](/en/characters/stick-it-to-the-stickman-dark-knight/) covers the extended steps and unlock checkpoints.

### Saitama / Yellow Gym Character

The Saitama or yellow character starts in the main tower's Gym floor.

- YOKO wrote on August 20, 2025 that you unlock the yellow character "by unlocking the gym level for the tower and doing 100 push ups 100 sit ups and 100 squats a pop up will then appear saying you unlocked a character."
- Cchhey phrased the trigger as "Get up to the Gym floor in the main tower and do 100 on every mat on that floor."
- Cchhey also gave a navigation shortcut: "instead of trying to get all the way to the gym floor (its pretty high up), Press Ctrl + / to skip floors."

| Character | First trigger | Community tip | Start region |
|---|---|---|---|
| Batman / Night Shift | Destroy all red CEO cutouts, then answer the phone | Reset wanted level at the car wash | City map |
| Saitama / yellow gym character | Do 100 reps on every mat on the Gym floor | Use Ctrl + / to skip floors | Main tower Gym floor |

## Fulfillment Center Web-Slinger Unlock Steps

The fulfillment center character is the newest of the documented Stick It to the Stickman secret characters, and it requires a camera from a random present rather than a direct conversation with an NPC.

John-DVD's spoiler-free guide gives the most complete path. The start point is technically the city map, because you need to loot presents until a camera appears.

| Step | Action | Location note |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Open city presents until you get a camera | City map, random drop |
| 2 | Photograph every web with the camera | Up and left of the Fulfillment Center; far left of the Finance Tower; down and right of the Evaluation Center |
| 3 | Pick up the lasso after the phone icon appears | After the photos register |
| 4 | Lasso three marked criminals and bring them to the police station door | City police station |

John-DVD warned that "taking the photos can be buggy sometimes." Cchhey added a practical tip for the criminal escort step: "when getting the criminals, move cars to block the streets you have to walk down since the AI really likes driving into people at full speed."

The [Web-Slinger guide](/en/characters/stick-it-to-the-stickman-webslinger/) expands the step-by-step route and cleanup details.

## Neuralink and CEO: Community Reports and Conflicts

The final two Stick It to the Stickman secret characters are less stable in player reports. Treat these as leads, not guaranteed post-launch unlocks.

### Neuralink Secret Character

On September 24, 2025, The Wise Wolf asked about a Neuralink character that shows something like "missing translation"/error text. NoobGod427 replied with a player-reported method:

> "in the windows with scientists there will occasionally be someone not in a lab coat. using the student (or wahtever character has the teleport ability) teleport into the glass and kill the guy before he runs away that will give you the new character"

The start location is Neuralink, but the unlock may be tied to a rare NPC spawn inside the scientist windows.

### Disgraced CEO and Bunker CEO Confusion

The CEO reports conflict across time periods. On April 1, 2024, AAA Cannonball asked whether there were other secret characters "beside Saitama and the Disgraced CEO." Later, on September 9, 2024, doofus0510 said the Disgraced CEO is from "the mind training facility beta."

A more recent thread added more confusion. On October 4, 2025, Ein Rabe aus dem Osten reported rerolling Neuralink around 100 times without finding the CEO and described the text `<!-MISSING TRANSLATION [NOT IMPLEMENTED]-!>`. Tekhyla responded with one line: "CEO is in the bunker."

## FAQ

### How many Stick It to the Stickman secret characters are there?

Community guides and discussion threads document five leads, but not all are confirmed unlocks after launch. The safest view is that Batman, Saitama, and the fulfillment center web-slinger have repeatable steps, while the Neuralink and CEO unlocks are unresolved.

### Where do I start the Batman unlock?

Start in the city map. Destroy every red CEO cutout, then follow the marker to the phone and defeat the marked CEO targets. Some players also suggest unlocking the cinema first.

### Which secret character starts on the Gym floor?

The Saitama or yellow gym character starts in the main tower's Gym floor. Reach that floor and perform 100 reps on every mat, then watch for the unlock popup.

### Is the Neuralink secret character bugged?

Players report missing translation or "NOT IMPLEMENTED" text on the icon. NoobGod427 gave a teleport-kill method, but it is a community report rather than official confirmation.
