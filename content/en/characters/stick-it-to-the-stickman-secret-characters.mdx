## Requirements Before You Hunt

Every secret character quest assumes you have progressed the main game. The night shift characters need night shift access, the Fulfillment Center characters need the center unlocked, and the Blood-gated ones need Blood Donation purchased first. Community threads repeat this warning because hunters routinely show up missing a prerequisite.

Treat prerequisites as your shopping list. Unlock the zone, buy the relevant upgrade, and only then start the quest steps, or you will spend a run chasing a trigger that cannot appear yet. The night shift requirement is the one hunters miss most, because night shift access is itself a mid-to-late unlock, and community threads call it a hard gate: no access, no Dark Knight quest, no exceptions.

| Secret hunt | Prerequisite to check first |
| --- | --- |
| Dark Knight | Night shift unlocked |
| Fulfillment Center character | Fulfillment Center unlocked |
| Vampire | Blood Donation upgrade |
| Saitama | Gym unlocked |

## How to Hunt Without Spoiling Yourself

Pick one secret character, read its spoiler-free guide once, and hunt with the guide closed. Community hunters report that reading step by step mid-run kills the fun, while reading once and then exploring keeps it. One more community habit: keep a note file per hunt, because writing down what you have tried prevents the classic loop of repeating the same wrong search for an hour.

If you get truly stuck, the full-spoiler guides exist too. The community maintains both styles, and the [saved spoiler-free guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3566329383) is the model to follow for a first attempt at the Fulfillment Center character, while the full guides are the fallback when a hunt stops being fun.

The saved community sources document several, including Saitama, the Dark Knight, Spider-Man, the Vampire, and a Fulfillment Center secret character with its own spoiler-free guide. The exact roster changes with updates, so check recent guides.

Yes. Night shift characters need night shift access, Fulfillment Center characters need the center, and the Vampire needs the Blood Donation upgrade. Missing prerequisites is the most common stall, and the fastest check before any Stick It to the Stickman secret characters hunt is the prerequisites list.

### Where do Stick It to the Stickman secret characters quests end?

Most end at a specific location, often the Fulfillment Center, where the character finally joins your roster after the final step. The Fulfillment Center appears as the final stop for several of them, which is why so many Stick It to the Stickman secret characters guides center on that zone.
