**Stick It to the Stickman is a Steam game for Windows and macOS.** As of 2026-10-18, the official Steam page lists those two operating systems and nothing else, and Free Lives' Steam news posts announce no PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo Switch or mobile version. If you want to play it today, a Steam account and a Windows or Mac machine is the route.

## PC (Steam)

The game left Early Access and hit 1.0 on September 23, 2026, after launching in Early Access on August 18, 2025. On 2026-10-18, the official Steam page listed the game at $11.99, and it was also sold in bundles — notably one paired with *Stick Fight: The Game*.

Worth knowing before you buy, because these are the features that decide whether a "platform" question actually matters to you:

- Single-player, plus shared/split-screen co-op on one machine.
- Remote Play Together, so a friend can join over Steam without owning a second copy locally.
- Steam Workshop support and an included level editor.
- Steam Cloud, achievements, and Family Sharing.

The Windows requirements are modest at the low end: a GTX 1050 Ti / RX 570 class card, 8 GB RAM and 6 GB of storage for 1080p at 30 FPS on Low. The recommended tier asks for an RTX 2080 / RX 6600 XT and 16 GB for 1080p at 60 FPS on High.

One caveat on the level editor, straight from the developer's December 2025 update: creating levels requires a keyboard and mouse, and Level Builder levels were not available for two players at that point.

## macOS

The Mac version is listed on the same Steam page, but the posted requirements are unusually steep for a stickman brawler: macOS 13.3.1 (Ventura) with an Apple M1 Max and **32 GB of RAM** as the minimum, for 1080p at 30 FPS on Low. That reads like a build tested on one high-end machine rather than a broad compatibility statement, so if you are on a base-model Mac, treat the listed minimum as the bar the developer published rather than a guarantee your machine will clear.

## PS5

No PS5 version has been announced. As of 2026-10-18, the official Steam page lists only Windows and macOS, and the developer's Steam news posts through the 1.0 launch and the October 2026 patch notes discuss PC updates only. There is no release window to report, and guessing one would be inventing information.

## Xbox

Same answer: as of 2026-10-18, the official Steam page does not list an Xbox version and Free Lives has not announced one in its Steam news feed. The Series X|S and Xbox One are not covered.

## Nintendo Switch

As of 2026-10-18, the official Steam page does not list a Switch version, and no Switch announcement appears in the developer's Steam news posts. The game's split-screen co-op and short-session structure make it a natural fit for the console, which is likely why the question comes up, but "would fit well" is not an announcement.

## Steam Deck

There is no Steam Deck compatibility rating stated in the store page's system requirements, which cover Windows and macOS. Deck status is a separate Valve label shown on the store page, so check the badge there rather than assuming from the PC requirements — a game can run fine on Windows and still carry no Deck rating at all. If you are weighing a purchase for handheld play, that badge is the thing to look for, and Remote Play Together is a Steam feature, not evidence of Deck support.

## Mobile

As of 2026-10-18, the official Steam page does not list an iOS or Android version, and nothing in the developer's Steam news posts announces one. There is no mobile build to point to.

## About the console rumours

A Steam community thread from April 2024 asked directly whether the game would come to consoles. The replies — including the claim that the game would arrive "on all platforms" and then stop being updated — are player speculation, not developer statements, and the thread contains no reply from Free Lives. The comparison to *Grounded* in that thread is one player's analogy, not a schedule. Treat the whole thread as fans thinking out loud about a game that was still in Early Access at the time.

The reliable place to watch is the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/Stick_It_to_the_Stickman/) and the [Steam news feed](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2085540), where Free Lives posts updates and any platform news would appear first.
