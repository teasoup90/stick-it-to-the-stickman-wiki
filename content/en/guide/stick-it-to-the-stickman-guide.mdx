## What you're actually doing

You are a new hire at The Company, and your job description is violence. Stick It to the Stickman is a rogue-lite physics brawler from Free Lives (published by Devolver Digital) in which you punch, kick, staple and chainsaw your way up the corporate ladder, chaining combos and picking from a large pool of moves, weapons and superpowers between fights.

Each run ends the same way: you reach CEO, the board immediately demotes you back to intern, and you start climbing again — the official Steam page makes the loop explicit rather than treating it as a fail state. What carries between runs is the unlock layer: new worker archetypes (Temps, Caffeine Addicts and Ego Maniacs are the ones the store page names) and additional game modes, which is why a run that ends badly still moves you forward.

The game left Early Access with its 1.0 release on 23 September 2026, and the official Steam page sells it at $11.99 with a demo available to download.

## Practical tips

1. **Use Cage Fight as your tutorial.** Free Lives' patch notes for 4 September 2025 describe reworking the on-ramp into Cage Fight and nerfing and tutorialising its boss. If you are still learning how moves chain together, that is the mode built to hold your hand.

2. **Know where the Unstuck button is.** The patch notes for 13 October 2026 added an "Unstuck" button to every game mode. Physics brawlers trap players in geometry, and the same notes list softlock fixes in Fulfillment, Neurolink and the final boss fight — so if a run freezes you in place, look for that button before assuming the save is dead.

3. **Pick a chain you can land, not a pile of moves.** The store page frames combat around chaining combos, and several patches have adjusted specific move chains rather than single attacks. Spending your choices on moves that flow into each other beats collecting impressive one-offs you never connect with.

4. **Read the perk interactions.** Steam Community guide author John-DVD writes about "run type" perks that change how you move and combine with each other — for example, having both speed walk and loosen up produces a different run than either alone. It is a player observation rather than a documented system, but it is a good reason to read what a perk does before you stack it.

5. **Check each mode's own archetype screen.** Archetypes are unlocked per mode rather than from one global menu. The 11 September 2025 patch notes gave Fulfillment its own archetype selection screen with Survivalist, Collector and a redacted third, and the Foreign Manufacturing notes point to an Expansion Screen for its five new archetypes.

6. **Bring a friend, with caveats.** The Steam page lists shared/split screen co-op and Remote Play Together. If you want custom content in multiplayer, note that the 4 December 2025 Level Builder notes state that builder levels are not available for two players.

7. **Only build levels with a keyboard and mouse.** The same December 2025 notes state that level creation requires a keyboard and mouse and that Level Builder shipped English-only, with localisation planned for later.

8. **Don't wait for the cut modes.** Free Lives' 1.0 announcement on 23 September 2026 states plainly that teased modes including the Marketing Department, the Robot Takeover of Fulfillment, the Science Center and Employee Evaluation Trials were left unfinished so the team could ship. If you are holding out for one of those, the announcement is the place to check rather than the roadmap.

## The game modes

**The Tower (main run).** The core corporate-ladder climb through the office building, and the mode most patches touch for balance and visual polish. Day Shift is its default flavour; the 30 October 2025 patch added new trap rooms to it.

**Night Shift.** An after-hours variant added on 30 October 2025 with themed floors and visitors including Killer Clowns, Nosferatu and Accountants. A 30 September 2025 hotfix addressed a bug that had forced players into Night Shift only.

**Cage Fight.** The stripped-down fighting mode used to teach the combat system, complete with its own boss and its own move and passive pool.

**Fulfillment Center.** Warehouse work: OSHA-violating storage, rogue forklifts and a dedicated archetype screen. The 11 September 2025 restructuring gave it Survivalist, Collector and a secret third archetype.

**Foreign Manufacturing.** A large expansion mode released 4 May 2026 and unlocked through the Stockmarket, adding five archetypes (the Ninja and Negotiator arrived in the 14 August 2026 update), ranged weapon twists, new bosses and an overtime variant.

**Company History Month.** A story-focused mode released 4 February 2026 covering the Founder's rise from orphaned newborn to businessman.

**Neurolink.** Shipped with 1.0 and built around brain-chip implants and telekinetic artillery. Community guide author MrMee documents a fourth secret archetype, the Disgraced CEO, tied to the Neurolink Facility.

**The finale.** The 1.0 run ends at the bottom of a billionaire's bunker against the Founder, in what the developer calls the game's ultimate boss fight.

**Level Builder.** Custom towers, enemies and props, shared through the Steam Workshop, added 4 December 2025.
