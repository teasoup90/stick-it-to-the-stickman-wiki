## How the Run Structure Works

Each run throws you back into an office full of coworkers, middle managers, and bosses, and your job is to fight upward. You keep collecting new moves and currencies between fights, and when you lose, the run ends but your unlocked progression stays. The core loop is therefore learn, spend, and climb, repeated until you can beat the final boss with a build you assembled yourself.

## The Upgrade and Unlock Order That Matters

Community reports consistently suggest spending early resources on things that open new content rather than raw stat bumps. A new zone, character, or move slot creates more options on every future run, while a small damage boost only helps the current one. The same logic applies to the resource currencies: blood, sweat, and the water drops all feed specific upgrade paths, so hoarding the wrong one stalls your climb.

Your first goal should be reaching and unlocking the zones in order, because later zones drop the currencies earlier zones cannot. Players who rush straight damage often find themselves stuck on an upgrade that requires a resource they never learned to farm. A Stick It to the Stickman guide cannot fix a bad unlock order after the fact, so spend deliberately from the start.

| Priority | Upgrade type | Why it comes first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | New zones and characters | Unlocks more content and resource drops |
| Medium | Move slots and combos | Makes every run smoother |
| Low | Small stat boosts | Only helps the current run |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

The biggest reported mistake is spending currencies on low-value upgrades before opening new zones. The second is ignoring the hub and the wiki entirely, then asking questions the community has already answered. The third is treating every community claim as fact; guides written before the latest patch sometimes describe removed or changed mechanics.

Finally, do not hoard resources forever. Currencies exist to be spent, and the climb accelerates when each run starts with better tools than the last one did. If a run feels stuck, the problem is usually an unlock you skipped, not a skill issue, so re-read this Stick It to the Stickman guide and check your progression order before blaming your reflexes.

The most common mistakes, in one list:

- Buying raw damage before opening new zones
- Skipping the hub and wiki and getting stuck on known questions
- Treating outdated early access guides as current patch notes
- Hoarding currencies instead of spending them on progression
