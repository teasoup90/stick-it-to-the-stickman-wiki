## Which achievements are hardest?

By the 2026-10-18 global unlock rates, the rarest five are **An arm and a leg** (2.5%), **Where it is due** (3.0%), **Off the spectrum** (4.2%), **You are better than Elon Musk** (4.5%), and **Guns don't kill people** (5.4%). Rarity is not the same as mechanical difficulty: **An arm and a leg** is a 1,000-dismemberment grind, while **Guns don't kill people** is a one-run constraint.

The long-tail tasks are the 1,000-count achievements (**Generational Pain**, **An arm and a leg**), the 100-count tasks (**Windows Update**, **up the corporate ladder**, **Wow, so much content**), and the streak achievements (**Fists of retrenchment**, **Feet of Retrenchment**). According to the community guide, a different attack can reset the punch streak, so keep the chain clean if you are going for **Fists of retrenchment**.

## Watch the wording

Some community guides translate or paraphrase achievement names. Use the Steam wording when matching a target: **up the corporate ladder** is climb 100 ladders, and **Fists of retrenchment** is 100 consecutive punches. If a guide adds a boss kill to the punch achievement, that is extra to the official condition. The same applies to **un-fired** and **Red-gicide**, where the official text is broad and the player routes fill in the steps.

## FAQ

### How many achievements does Stick It to the Stickman have?
Steam's global achievement page header says 40 achievements (23 rare) as of 2026-10-18; its public list shows 26 with unlock rates, and those are the ones covered here.

### Which Stick It to the Stickman achievement is rarest?
On 2026-10-18, **An arm and a leg** is lowest at 2.5%, followed by **Where it is due** at 3.0% and **Off the spectrum** at 4.2%.

### How do I unlock Fists of retrenchment?
Land 100 consecutive punches. A community guide recommends the "weeb" character with maxed Karate Punch and warns that other attacks reset the streak.

### Why do some guides give different achievement names or conditions?
Community guides paraphrase or translate. For example, **up the corporate ladder** is climb 100 ladders, and **Fists of retrenchment** is 100 consecutive punches, not a punch count plus a separate boss kill.
