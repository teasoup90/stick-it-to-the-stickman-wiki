## The Progression Achievements

The guide's first milestone is the rooftop boss, tied to the achievement 'You can't fire me, I'm leaving myself'. Killing that boss a second time awards 'Entering the stock exchange', which means your first two achievements are the same fight twice.

From there the route opens up: 'Learn to drive' asks you to rise to the level shown on the exchange and complete the training there. Completing that training also unlocks rated battles, which is the trigger for 'Fighting with hands behind your back'.

Community players add one warning about the second boss kill: it must happen after the exchange content is available, so do not grind the rooftop boss before the story sends you there. The guide's order exists precisely to avoid such wasted kills.

| --- | --- |
| You can't fire me, I'm leaving myself | Defeat the rooftop boss the first time |
| Entering the stock exchange | Kill the boss a second time |
| Learn to drive | Reach the exchange level and finish training |
| Fighting with hands behind your back | Open rated battles after training |

## The Exploration Achievements

For 'Climb the career ladder' the guide simply says: climb 100 stairs. It sounds trivial until you realize it is tracked across runs, so the community tip is to include stair climbs in every route you already run.

'Oriented' comes from the Fulfillment Center: go up to the level shown on the exchange, where the center opens, and play in it. 'We only go out at night' is the night shift version: climb the exchange to the level shown and play the night shift with any character. Players who miss the stair counter early often finish it last, so the community habit is to climb every staircase you see from run one.

## The Character and Gimmick Achievements

Some achievements lock onto specific characters. 'Unfired' belongs to the task dodger: pump the paper airplane to its maximum level to get a fire extinguisher, then put out a burning colleague with it. The guide notes this is much easier with a friend who has any burning attack, and that the cage fights mode with a cigar and fire extinguisher also works.

'Disabled' is the cheeky one: refuse work several times at the very start of the game until you die. 'Fists of Dismissal' wants 100 hits with your hands only, and any other attack resets the counter, then you must defeat the boss with that 100-hit streak. The guide recommends the weeb character with karate punch maxed, on a new save for the easiest setup. The guide also lists 'Color profiling', which community reports tie to character color unlocks, and 'Red regicide', which overlaps with the dark knight secret character quest from the secrets guides.

## FAQ

### How do I get Fists of Dismissal?

Land 100 consecutive hits using only your hands, then beat the boss with the streak intact. The community route is the weeb character with karate punch maxed on a new save. The streak resets if you attack with anything else, which is why the new save matters, and it is the one unlock in the Stick It to the Stickman achievement guide most players save for last.

### Does the Stick It to the Stickman achievement guide require co-op?

No, but 'Unfired' is much easier with a friend using a burning attack. You can also do it solo in cage fights with a cigar and a fire extinguisher. The cage fight method works when no friend is around.

### How do I unlock the night shift achievement?

Rise to the level shown on the exchange and play the night shift with any character to unlock 'We only go out at night'. The exchange climb is the same step the training achievements use.

### What is the easiest achievement?

Community players name 'Disabled' as the easiest: refuse work several times at the start of the game until you die. No combat skill is required, just menu patience.
