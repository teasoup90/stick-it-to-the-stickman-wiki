## What Is the Stick It to the Stickman Ending?

The stick it to the stickman ending is the payoff for a very long climb. You punch, kick, staple, and chainsaw your way up a hostile corporate ladder, and the finale answers one question: what happens when an intern finally reaches the top? Because the game evolved from Early Access into 1.0, there are actually two documented endings, and this guide separates the community-documented demo version from the finished release.

Be warned that the saved sources for this article describe the ending in fragments. The official [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/) and the developer news posts cover the structure of the finale, while the community wiki fills in the choice at the top. Anything from the demo era is labelled below, and anything not documented at all is called out honestly.

## The Demo-Era Ending the Community Documented

The community wiki, written during version 0.78 of the Early Access period, describes how a run used to conclude. After climbing the building, the player reaches the boss, a final battle ensues, and upon defeating him the Shareholders give the player a choice: become the new CEO, or refuse. Choosing no means being struck by lightning; choosing yes means becoming the new boss and being allowed to purchase upgrades for the office.

The same wiki page also documents an early alternate path. The game starts with the player choosing to sign a series of contracts to get a job, and choosing not to work produces an alternate synopsis in which the player's family starves. Community pages treat that as a joke ending rather than a full route, and it is only described for the Early Access build.

This wiki information predates the full release and reflects demo-era behavior (the wiki labels its own version as 0.78). It is the best community documentation we have, but the 1.0 release changed the finale, as the next section explains.

## The 1.0 Finale: The Founder's Bunker

The finished game moves the finale underground. The official store description says you end the corporate climb by racing to the bottom of a doomsday bunker to fight the one and only Founder. The 1.0 launch news from Free Lives confirms it: the near-final update includes the game's ultimate boss fight, at the bottom of a billionaire's bunker, alongside the new Neurolink gamemode. The developers described 1.0 as a hefty chunk of new gameplay arriving in a near-final update, and they were open about the fact that the team could not finish everything it had started.

Before launch, the press framed it the same way. A Rock Paper Shotgun article shared through the official Steam news feed in August 2026 previewed the game leaving Early Access with neurolinkage and an "absolutely absurd finale". Developer news also confirmed the release window: the game entered 1.0 on September 23, 2026, after an Early Access launch on August 18, 2025.

Community players have already mapped the ending achievements. A comment in the top Steam achievement guide reports that "Look at this Big man" requires finishing the bunker and refusing the crown, and that "Dude come on" requires continuing to play after that refusal. Those reports line up with the older choice structure, updated for the bunker, and they make the 1.0 stick it to the stickman ending something completionists can chase in a single evening.

What is not yet documented: the exact sequence of the 1.0 ending cutscene, whether multiple endings exist in 1.0, and anything about an ending song. The popular search for a stick it to the stickman ending song is not covered by any of the saved sources, so treat song claims as unverified for now.

## What the Ending Means for Your Next Run

The stick it to the stickman ending is not really an ending, which fits the rogue-lite loop. The official store page warns that once you have laid waste to your rivals and been anointed CEO, the board will instantly demote you back to being an intern. As the developers put it, you learned how to tie a tie for nothing, and it is time to clock in again.

That loop is the point. Each run funds upgrades for the next one, and the finale is a milestone rather than a stopping point. Community achievement hunters treat the bunker refusal as a checkpoint worth doing at least twice, once for "Look at this Big man" and once for "Dude come on". The demo-era pattern survives in spirit: you win the fight, you make a choice, and then the machine of office life simply starts again. For new players, the practical takeaway is to treat the first clear as practice, because the demotion loop expects you to climb more than once.

## FAQ

**How do you unlock the stick it to the stickman ending?**
Beat the final fight of a run. In the 1.0 release the developers say the ultimate boss fight sits at the bottom of a billionaire's bunker, and the store page calls that final opponent the Founder. There is no secret flag to flip: reaching the finale is the natural endpoint of a successful tower run, with the bunker acting as the game's last arena.

**What happens if you refuse the CEO role?**
The community wiki says refusing the Shareholders' offer means being struck by lightning. Achievement guide comments add that in 1.0 you can finish the bunker and refuse the crown, and that continuing to play after that refusal unlocks another achievement, so refusal is treated as a valid route rather than a dead end.

**Does the game have multiple endings?**
The demo-era wiki documents an early alternate synopsis for refusing to work, plus the CEO choice at the top of the tower. The saved sources do not confirm how many distinct endings exist in the 1.0 release, and the developers have kept the post-bunker details vague in their news posts.

**Is there a stick it to the stickman ending song?**
None of the saved sources mention an ending song. Until official materials or reliable community pages document one, treat any song claims as unverified.
