**Short answer:** the community fix for a controller that won't control player 2 is a lobby workaround, but the author of that thread later said it was a beta-era trick and that the game had added a way to assign an input device to each player. The original steps, posted on July 6, 2024, were:

1. Be in the lobby where you can add another player.
2. Press I to put two players in the game.
3. Use the kill control on player 1.
4. Respawn player 1.

The poster's claim is that the controller then works as player 2's device. The later update is the part to check first on the Steam version you are running; the thread does not name the menu or screen where device assignment happens.

## What changed after the beta

The thread is older than the 1.0 release. The [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/Stick_It_to_the_Stickman/) lists Early Access on August 18, 2025 and full release on September 23, 2026. The last reply shown in the thread, from October 19, 2025, says the guide was for the beta and is "now useless" because input devices can be assigned to each player.

That reply is newer than the lobby trick, but it is not a 1.0 walkthrough and it does not give a menu path. Treat the numbered steps as the historical workaround and the per-player assignment option as the first thing to look for.

## The disconnect-and-reconnect variant

A reply from April 13, 2025 said the original method did not work for that player and offered a variant: get player 1 and player 2 into the game with I, disconnect the connected controller for a moment, then plug it back in. The poster also mentioned going to "the tab" before reconnecting, without explaining which tab. This is a single player report, not a confirmed sequence for 1.0, but it is another reported thing to try if the original four steps do not take.
