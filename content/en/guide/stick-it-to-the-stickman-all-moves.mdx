## The Ranged and Tool Moves

The category's ranged side covers Gun and Machine gun, plus the item-flavored tools Delivery Parcel and Mail Bomb. Community reports describe ranged moves as your answer to enemies that punish melee, and the parcel moves as area denial that works even mid-combo.

Ranged moves consume resources or have cooldowns, so the community habit is one ranged move per loadout, never three. A machine gun clears rooms, but it cannot be your only answer to a boss. The wiki's navigation lists Ranged Move separately, and community threads treat ranged as a support slot rather than a primary style, because enemies close distance fast in this game.

Delivery Parcel and Mail Bomb are the wildcards: community reports describe parcel setups that stall entire rooms while a melee partner cleans up, and Mail Bomb as a finisher with setup time. Both reward planning more than reflexes.

## FAQ

### Where does the Stick It to the Stickman all moves list come from?

It comes from the game's community wiki Move category page, which lists 33 move items in total along with related sections for guns and ranged moves. New moves appear with patches, so the list grows over time.

### Which move is best for bosses?

Community reports name Serious Punch as the favorite boss-killer, paired with Roll to reposition between the boss's big swings. Bring Roll to dodge the big swings and punish the recovery, and the fight becomes a rhythm instead of a race.

### Are the joke moves actually useful?

Yes. Players report Fart, Gossip, and Ponder all have real applications, especially Fart for knockback and area denial in crowded rooms. The community joke is that they are why the game got made, and every one of them still shows up in serious builds.

### How many moves should a loadout have?

The community formula is four: one poke, one heavy, one crowd control tool, and one ranged answer, which covers every room type without redundant damage. It also leaves room to experiment with one wildcard.
