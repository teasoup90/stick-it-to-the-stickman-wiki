## What the Official Sources Confirm

The official Steam page describes a rogue-lite physics brawler where you fight up the corporate ladder, and the developers have expanded it into several modes. This stick it to the stickman walkthrough covers the confirmed campaign flow, key unlocks, secret characters, and the 1.0 finale without inventing floor details that the saved sources do not provide.

The [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/) calls Stick It to the Stickman "a rogue-lite physics brawler where you brutally displace your way up the corporate ladder." Developed by Free Lives and published by Devolver Digital, it left Early Access on September 23, 2026. A complete stick it to the stickman walkthrough therefore has to cover more than one climb: the [official Steam news feed](https://api.steampowered.com/ISteamNews/GetNewsForApp/v2/?appid=2085540&count=20&maxlength=0&format=json) lists Day Shift, Night Shift, Cage Fight, Fulfillment Center, Foreign Manufacturing, Company History Month, Neurolink, and a grand final mode.

## Main Campaign and Mode Progression

The core of any stick it to the stickman walkthrough starts with the game's stated job: "As a new employee, your primary responsibility is to physically depose your supervisors, fight your way to the top of the corporate ladder (literally, with violence), and claim the ultimate title of CEO." To do that, you chain together combos and choose from moves, weapons, and superpowers while unlocking new office archetypes.

A practical stick it to the stickman walkthrough should also flag progression problems that the developers fixed before or around 1.0. The August 14, 2026 news update notes fixes for progression in the Employee Evaluation centre, infinite promotions in Fulfillment, ultrawide trap rooms, boss arena softlocks, and enemies spawning in the player's face. Those were exact softlock or progression issues that could stop a run, so they are worth knowing if you play an older build.

## FAQ

### Is there a complete stick it to the stickman walkthrough for every floor?

Not in the saved sources. The official Steam news and store page confirm the overall goal, main modes, and key unlocks, but they do not publish a floor-by-floor route for every level. You may need a current community walkthrough for exact layouts.

### How do you unlock Foreign Manufacturing?

The May 4, 2026 Steam news announcement says Foreign Manufacturing can be unlocked through the Stockmarket. It added five new archetypes, new twists on buildcraft and ranged weaponry, and new enemies in unique locations.

### What does the 1.0 update add to the walkthrough route?

The September 23, 2026 1.0 release added the Neurolink gamemode and the game's ultimate boss fight at the bottom of a billionaire's bunker. The developers also said some teased modes would not be finished, so the final route focuses on the shipping modes.

### Are secret character steps official?

No. The Batman, Saitama, Spiderman, and One Mighty Egg unlock steps come from community guides posted in 2025. They can be useful, but they are not official instructions and may change.
