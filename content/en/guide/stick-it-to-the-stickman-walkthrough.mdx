## The Route in Overview

The climb has four big phases: learning the office, opening the stock exchange content, surviving the Fulfillment Center and Finance Tower, and clearing the night shift endgame. Each phase gates the next one behind a boss or a resource requirement, so skipping ahead is rarely possible.

The overview table below lists each phase with the goal that ends it. Community threads describe players who rush the final zones as the ones who hit the hardest walls, because the middle zones fund the upgrades the endgame assumes you already own.

The community's most consistent piece of walkthrough wisdom is to finish each phase before sampling the next. A half-finished mid game sends you into the night shift with a weak build, and the resulting losses teach nothing except that the route order matters.

## First Clear: Rooftop and Stock Exchange

Your first objective is the rooftop boss, the fight the community's achievement guide treats as the game's first milestone. Community reports describe it as a pattern check: the boss telegraphs big swings, and the punish-after-dodge habit carries you through with basic moves alone.

After the rooftop, the stock exchange opens. The all-achievements guide describes the next milestones there: defeat the boss a second time, then complete the training tied to the exchange level. Completing that training also unlocks rated battles, which the guide frames as the bridge to mid game.

A Stick It to the Stickman walkthrough should also mention the opening unlock rhythm: community guides describe the early hours as a loop of fight, spend, return, where each boss clear funds one or two permanent upgrades. Skipping a return trip to spend is a classic early wall, and defeating the rooftop boss twice in a row without spending in between wastes the exact resources the exchange phase needs.

## Mid Game: Fulfillment Center and Finance Tower

With the exchange cleared, the Fulfillment Center becomes your base for resource unlocks. Community threads place the Blood Donation upgrade here, the requirement for farming blood, and the Finance Tower next to it as the zone for paperclips, sweat on its harder difficulty, and night shift content.

This is the farming stretch. The walkthrough advice that matters here is to unlock drop sources before spending: Blood Donation before blood, the Accounts Department upgrade before paperclip farming, and night shift access before ghosts. Players who invert that order report multi-run stalls. Treat the farming stretch as the route's backbone.

One more piece of walkthrough advice that threads repeat: farm with a friend. Community reports describe co-op as the fastest way to clear resource rooms, and a second player makes the sweat and paperclip farms noticeably shorter. Even solo players benefit from one co-op session per farm target.

| Mid game unlock | Why it comes first |
| --- | --- |
| Blood Donation | Enables blood drops for character unlocks |
| Accounts Department | Spawns paperclip-heavy floors |
| Night shift access | Unlocks ghosts and night-only content |
| Rated battles | Unlocks cage fight ranks |

## FAQ

### What order should I follow in a Stick It to the Stickman walkthrough?

Follow the phases in order: rooftop boss, stock exchange training, Fulfillment Center and Finance Tower farming, then the night shift endgame. If a phase feels impossible, you skipped a farm step, not a fight.

### Where do I farm resources in the mid game?

Community reports point to the Fulfillment Center for blood after Blood Donation, and the Finance Tower for paperclips, sweat on higher difficulty, and ghosts at night. If a room stops dropping, double-check that the drop upgrade is still purchased; community threads report losing it after updates.

### When do rated battles unlock?

The community's achievement guide says rated battles open after you complete the training tied to the stock exchange level. Community threads confirm it appears after the exchange training milestone.

### What should I do after the final clear?

Clean up achievements and secret characters using the hub's top-rated guides, especially the all-achievements guide and the secret character guide. Both are linked from the hub's guide categories.
