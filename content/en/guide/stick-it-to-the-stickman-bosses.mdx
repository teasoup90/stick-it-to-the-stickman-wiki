## Meet the Stick It to the Stickman Bosses

Stick It to the Stickman bosses are the headline fights of this rogue-lite physics brawler, and knowing their moves is the difference between a promotion and a pink slip. Free Lives built the game around one goal: physically depose your supervisors and fight your way to the top of the corporate ladder until you can claim the title of CEO. The community has cataloged most of these encounters, so you do not have to walk into the Manager's office blind.

The full picture is still a work in progress. The community wiki openly admits that not all bosses have images yet, and several descriptions come from the demo build rather than the finished 1.0 release. Where a fight is only documented from the demo, this guide says so clearly. Before you dive in, grab the basics from the [official Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/).

## Early Fights: Manager, Accounting Boss, Intern Boss

The Manager is described by the community as a possible first boss with 14 HP. His moves are force choke, slap, and shout, and he can spawn enemies when yelling "Out Smarted!". Community strategy notes say his force choke deals no damage; it stops you and stuns you for a second. The wiki lists his weakness as the machine gun, which takes him out in one hit, or any move able to do more than 14 damage. Player advice from the same page: rush at the boss and spam attacks, but do not get too close, or he will shout at you and possibly choke you.

The Accounting Boss is documented from the demo build. His main attacks are swinging his briefcase, Spin Punch, and Pontificate, and the briefcase releases papers that stun the player. He also summons Accounting Employees, tiny versions of him that cannot spin punch. Community notes call him very easy to kill, pointing out you can simply knock him out of a window; he has a fair amount of HP and does not give a lot of XP. The same page adds that he will eventually be replaced with the Chief Catering Officer with one of the upgrades, most likely the Cafeteria upgrade.

The Intern Boss is exclusive to the demo, according to the community wiki. He stands on top of a platform while interns do the fighting for him, and he shouts lines like "Bring on the Interns" before the fight and "MORE INTERNS" when low on health. Each call summons only three interns, and he can occasionally land a Spin Punch for 1 damage. One wiki contributor estimates his HP at around 50-60 but openly says they are not sure, so do not treat that number as fact.

## The Gym and Security Officers

The Chief Gyming Officer is a possible third boss with 60 health, a ground slam, and a basic punch. In newer versions he also carries a Staple Gun. The wiki notes he has been revamped three times, making him the most changed boss in the game. Community strategy is blunt: push him out the window or just beat him to death, because he is not a hard fight.

The Chief Security Officer is another possible third boss with 60 health. His kit is a punch, staple gun, shout, and taser gun. The wiki calls him similar to the Assistant Security Officer, just bigger, with extra tricks: he can charge, Stamp, Punch Both Sides, and Elbow Slam, which practically combines the Assistant and the Chief Gyming Officer.

Both officers sit in the middle of the roster, and because they are marked as possible third bosses, the order of your stick it to the stickman bosses run can vary between saves. That is one reason the community pages avoid hard numbers on progression and mark their data as incomplete.

## Tips for Beating the Stick It to the Stickman Bosses

- Read the moves before the fight: force choke, briefcase papers, and shouts all open clear punish windows.
- Bring burst damage: a machine gun one-shots the Manager, and any move over 14 damage skips his gimmick entirely.
- Control the adds: intern and accounting summons are finite, so clear them before focusing the boss.
- Use the windows: two community pages suggest knocking bosses out of a window as the fastest kill.
- Track your progress with achievements: defeating the rooftop boss for the first time unlocks "You can't fire me, I'm leaving myself", and killing the boss a second time unlocks "Entering the stock exchange".

## FAQ

**How many stick it to the stickman bosses are there?**
The community wiki lists thirteen named bosses, from the Manager up to Jeff Bezos, but it also warns the page is a work in progress and that not all bosses have images yet.

**Who is the first boss in the game?**
Community pages describe the Manager as a possible first boss with 14 HP and moves including force choke, slap, and shout.

**Does the Intern Boss appear in the full game?**
According to the community wiki, the Intern Boss is exclusive to the demo, so do not expect him in the 1.0 release unless future updates change that.

**What is the final fight of the game?**
The official Steam page describes racing to the bottom of a doomsday bunker to fight the one and only Founder, which community guides treat as the ultimate boss encounter of the 1.0 update.
