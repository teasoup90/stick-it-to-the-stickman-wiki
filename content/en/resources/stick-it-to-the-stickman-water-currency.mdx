The Stick It to the Stickman water currency is the resource players describe as the little water droplets used for upgrades, and it is the most genuinely under-documented currency in the saved corpus. The thread that asks about it gets a detailed answer about every other resource, while the droplets themselves stay mysterious.

That asymmetry is the story of this page. The community knows sweat, blood, paperclips, hats, and ghosts cold, but the water droplets are confirmed to exist, confirmed to be used for upgrades, and left without a farming method in the saved record.

This Stick It to the Stickman water currency guide documents exactly that state: what is confirmed, what is distinct, and what remains unknown, without inventing a method to fill the gap.

A note on scope: this page covers the droplets only. The other currencies get their own pages, and the water droplets get the careful treatment they deserve precisely because their documentation is so thin.

## The Teardrop Versus the Droplets

The question's contrast between the single teardrop thing and the multiple waterdrops is the clearest evidence that two distinct items exist. This page keeps them separate because merging them would misquote the source.

If you have the teardrop and are looking at an upgrade that wants droplets, the saved thread implies those are different balances, so do not assume one counts toward the other.

This distinction is the single most actionable fact on the page: before grinding for droplets, confirm which water item the upgrade menu actually shows, because the thread's question treats them as separate things.
