## How the Donation Loop Works

The loop is health for blood. Every few rooms, the game offers you the chance to donate health, and accepting it converts that health into blood. The trade is the entire mechanic, so the farming decision is always the same: how much health can you afford to give up?

Community reports frame it as a pacing choice rather than a freebie. If your build heals easily, donate aggressively. If your run is struggling, skip the chair and donate on the next offer, because the offers repeat as you move through the building.

One more practical point from the phrasing: the donation is an opportunity, not a demand. The game asks, and you decide, which means blood farming never has to kill a run unless you let greed do it.
