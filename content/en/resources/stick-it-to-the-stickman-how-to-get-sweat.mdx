## The Enemy That Drops Sweat

Sweat is dropped by sweaty construction workers. That enemy type is the entire source of sweat in the saved record, and the phrasing suggests the workers are visually distinct, though the thread does not describe their appearance beyond the name.

When one of these workers is defeated, he will drop sweat puddles that you have to touch to gain sweat. The touch step is the part players miss: the puddles sit on the ground after the kill, and walking over them is what actually banks the resource.

The saved wording treats the puddles as the resource itself, not the worker. The kill creates the puddles, but the touch creates the sweat balance, which is why a fast player can clear a room and still leave empty-handed.

| Enemy | Sweaty construction workers |
| Drop type | Sweat puddles on the ground |
| Pickup | Touch the puddles |
| Missed by | Players who walk past them |

## How to Farm Sweat Efficiently

The farming loop is simple: enter the named location, defeat the sweaty workers, and touch every puddle before moving on. The thread does not describe spawn rates, so treat each floor as a dice roll rather than a guaranteed count.

Community reports add one habit that helps: clear the room fully before looting, because the puddles stay where the workers fell, and picking them up mid-fight risks missing drops from workers you have not killed yet.

The same habit applies on the Finance Tower runs: on the $15 difficulty the workers share floors with other threats, so finish the fight first, then sweep the floor for puddles.

| Farming step | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Pick the right location | Workers spawn in two named places |
| Finish the fight | Kills trigger the drops |
| Touch every puddle | Pickup is manual |
| Sweep the floor | Drops stay where workers fell |

## Sweat Compared to Blood

Sweat and blood both gate progression upgrades, but the methods are opposites. Sweat is a combat drop from specific enemies, while blood comes from donating health at a station in the Fulfillment Center.

That means a run can be great for sweat and useless for blood, or the reverse. If an upgrade needs both, plan two separate loops instead of expecting one route to cover them.

| Resource | How you get it |

| Sweat | Defeat sweaty workers, touch puddles |
| Blood | Donate health at the center |
| Paperclips | Break boxes in the Finance Tower |
| Hat | Defeat the CEO with a new character or difficulty |
