## Paperclips and the Accounts Department

The core Stick It to the Stickman finance tower upgrade loop starts with paperclips. The saved thread says to break paperclip boxes in the Finance Tower to get paperclips, which makes the building the paperclip farm of the game.

The same reply adds the key upgrade detail: the Accounts Department upgrade will spawn floors with many paperclips. That is the upgrade most players want, because it converts the Finance Tower from a place where paperclips trickle in to a place where entire floors are packed with them, and it pays for itself over the next few runs.

New players often miss the boxes entirely because they read the Finance Tower as a combat area, but the paperclip economy is why the building stays relevant long after its fights become routine.

## Sweat and the Higher Difficulty Floors

Sweat is the second resource tied to the Finance Tower. The saved thread says sweat is dropped by sweaty construction workers, primarily in the Fulfillment Center and on the $15 difficulty in the Finance Tower.

That difficulty note matters for planning: if you are pushing a Stick It to the Stickman finance tower upgrade that costs sweat, running the $15 difficulty floors is the intended farm. When one of these workers is defeated, he drops sweat puddles that you have to touch to gain sweat, so the pickup step is manual, not automatic.

The thread does not say how much sweat each worker drops, so plan your runs around the location rather than around a specific amount per fight.

| Sweat facts | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Source | Sweaty construction workers |
| Main location | Fulfillment Center |
| Finance Tower spot | $15 difficulty floors |
| Pickup | Touch the dropped puddles |

## Night Shift and Ghosts

The Finance Tower also hosts the Night Shift, and the saved thread confirms it is the only place to obtain ghosts. The enemies with halos can die twice: defeat them once and they become gray and float around, and defeat them again to absorb their ghosts.

This matters for the upgrade question because several late upgrade paths use ghosts, and the Night Shift is the only confirmed source. If a Stick It to the Stickman finance tower upgrade list asks for ghosts, the route runs through the Finance Tower at night, and nowhere else in the saved record.

Because the haloed enemies are the only ghost source named in the saved thread, any ghost farm has to run Night Shift; the thread names no alternative.

| Where | Night Shift at the Finance Tower |
| Which enemies | The ones with halos |

## FAQ

### What is the best Stick It to the Stickman finance tower upgrade?

The Accounts Department upgrade is the one the saved thread names directly, because it spawns floors with many paperclips and turns the tower into a proper farm.

### How do I get paperclips in the Finance Tower?

Break paperclip boxes inside the Finance Tower. The saved thread states this plainly as the paperclip method.

### Where do I get sweat for Finance Tower upgrades?

Defeat sweaty construction workers on the $15 difficulty in the Finance Tower or in the Fulfillment Center, then touch the sweat puddles they drop.

### Why is the Finance Tower wiki page not quoted here?

The saved capture of the wiki page was blocked by a security verification page, so this article only uses the Finance Tower facts other saved sources confirm.

### What are ghosts used for?

The saved thread confirms ghosts come only from Night Shift enemies with halos, but it does not list every upgrade that spends them.

### Does the Finance Tower have floors beyond the $15 difficulty?

The saved thread only names the $15 difficulty for the sweat farm, so higher or lower floors are not claimed on this page.

### Is the Accounts Department upgrade worth it before anything else?

If paperclips are your bottleneck, yes: it spawns floors packed with boxes, and the saved thread presents it as the paperclip upgrade without any competing option.
