The Cage Fight ladder in *Stick It to the Stickman* is an **online-mode** progression. The portion of it players agree on is **Snake → Wolf → Tiger → Demon**, with Demon usually described as the fourth rank. You move up by winning online Cage Fight matches rather than by playing the single-player campaign, and Demon is the rank most people care about because a Foreign Manufacturing unlock sits behind it.

One caveat before the list: the player posts below do not state what comes before Snake, or confirm that Snake is the first rank. Treat the sequence as the stretch players have actually reported.

## The rank order players report

- **Snake**
- **Wolf**
- **Tiger**
- **Demon**

In a [Steam Community thread titled "Demon Rank"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2085540/discussions/0/569288789836524447/), a player who had just started online play asked how far away the rank was and was told it was "about 3 ranks up from snake. goes snake, wolf, tiger, demon." In another thread, a player answered the same question more briefly: Demon is "the rank right after tiger." A third poster, replying in a thread about whether online mode was working, put it as a number: "You need at least rank 4 (Demon) to unlock a class in another center."

Those three accounts line up well enough to be useful, but they are player descriptions, not a developer-published table.

## How you actually rank up

- **It happens in online Cage Fight matches.** Players repeatedly point out that the requirement is tied to the online version of the mode, and one player who asked whether single-player play would do was told it would not.
- **Promotion is tied to winning sets of fights.** One explanation is that you gain a rank after defeating six sets of other players' characters. That figure comes from a single player and should be treated as an estimate, not a rule.
- **A hot streak can skip a rank.** One player warns that if you are winning too consistently, a streak can carry you straight past an entire rank. That matters if you are chasing one specific rank for an unlock rather than climbing for its own sake.

Because these posts span the game's life — one unanswered thread dates from September 23, 2025, during Early Access, while others come after the September 23, 2026 full release — and none of them name a build number, exact promotion counts may have shifted between versions. The general shape of "win online fights, gain ranks" is consistent across all of them.

## What Demon rank is for

Demon is not just a badge. The Foreign Manufacturing centre has an unlock that requires reaching [Demon rank](/en/progression/stick-it-to-the-stickman-demon-rank/) in Cage Fight, and at least one class in another centre is reported to need "at least rank 4 (Demon)." This is the source of a recurring complaint in the discussions: players who bought the game for its single-player brawling resent having to grind an online auto-battler to open single-player content. If you are only after the unlock, plan for several online sessions rather than a quick detour.

## Does the ladder stop at Demon?

This is genuinely unsettled in the player reports. A [thread asking "How many Ranks is there to Cage Fight?"](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2085540/discussions/0/600790523214561546/) posed exactly that question — whether it stops at Demon and loops — and received no replies.

There is also one conflicting detail: a player describing how win streaks skip ranks says that if you skip Demon, you land on "the one before dragon rank," which implies a rank called Dragon sits above Demon. That does not square with Demon being described elsewhere as rank 4. No player in these threads lays out a confirmed list beyond Demon, so do not assume Demon is the top of the ladder — and do not assume a specific rank follows it either.

## Practical notes

- **Expect instability.** Players in the online threads report getting stuck in arena geometry, a black screen after a round where movement could still be heard, and losing all progress in a run after being forced to quit. If a match hangs, this is a known complaint rather than something you did wrong; there is no confirmed fix in these threads.
- **Chase the unlock, not the ladder.** If Foreign Manufacturing is your goal, aim for Demon and stop worrying about what is above it until players document more.
- **An unconfirmed shortcut circulates.** In an October discussion, one player floated <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>G</kbd> as a god-mode toggle to speed the grind up, another claimed it worked in online matches too, and a third said pressing it did nothing. There is no developer documentation for this in the material, and the reports contradict each other — treat it as a rumor, not a working control.

## FAQ

### What are the Cage Fight ranks in Stick It to the Stickman?

Players report the order as Snake, Wolf, Tiger and Demon, with Demon described as the fourth rank. Nobody in the discussions documents what comes before Snake.

### Do you have to play online to get Demon rank?

Yes, according to the players answering the question. The rank is earned in online Cage Fight matches; single-player play does not advance it.

### Is Demon the highest Cage Fight rank?

It is not confirmed either way. A September 23, 2025 thread asking whether the ladder stops at Demon went unanswered, and one player's mention of a "dragon rank" above Demon conflicts with other posts calling Demon rank 4.

### How many wins does it take to climb a Cage Fight rank?

One player estimates a rank is awarded after defeating six sets of other players' characters, and notes that a long win streak can skip an entire rank. That is a player estimate, not a published number.
