The Foreign Manufacturing upgrade screen in Stick It to the Stickman points you toward an objective that is easy to miss: you need the stick it to the stickman demon rank. That requirement is also an online-only goal, so the stick it to the stickman demon rank cannot be earned by grinding single-player fights. According to Steam Community reports from May 2025, you earn it by winning online Cage Fight matches and moving through a short animal-themed rank ladder.

## How to Get the Stick It to the Stickman Demon Rank

Start with Cage Fight online matches. A May 4, 2025 post by Spiderbat explains that in online mode, "there are ranks you get after defeating 6 sets of other player characters." That means the core loop is to win fights against player characters in sets, not to clear story missions. A May 5 post from najlitarvan calls the stick it to the stickman demon rank "like the 4th or so rank," and another player, jeffwakesk8, says it is "the rank right after tiger."

You do not have to win a specific story boss or complete a single-player run. The [official Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2085540/) for Stick It to the Stickman confirms the game is a rogue-lite physics brawler from Free Lives and Devolver Digital with online interactivity listed. Kenari wrote on Oct 1, 2025, "You need at least rank 4 (Demon) to unlock a class in another center," which confirms the upgrade that requires the stick it to the stickman demon rank.

## What the Stick It to the Stickman Demon Rank Unlocks

The main reason players chase the stick it to the stickman demon rank is a locked class upgrade in the Foreign Manufacturing center. Multiple Steam Community threads from spring and autumn 2025 link the same requirement to that single-player progression area. Spiderbat noted on May 4, 2025 that "one of the unlocks in Foreign Manufacturing requires you to get 'Demon Rank' in Cage Fight."

Kenari later gave a slightly more specific version on Oct 1, 2025: "You need at least rank 4 (Demon) to unlock a class in another center." That lines up with the rank order above, where Demon is listed as the fourth animal rank after Snake, Wolf, and Tiger. The available sources do not say which class unlocks, only that the requirement sits inside Foreign Manufacturing and is tied to online progression.

This design creates an unusual friction point. Stick It to the Stickman is built around solo brawling and workplace chaos, but the stick it to the stickman demon rank cannot be bypassed through normal single-player missions. Players who prefer offline play have to enter online Cage Fight at least long enough to climb to the fourth rank.

## Why the Demon Rank Requirement Splits Players

Some players are comfortable with Cage Fight as a side activity but dislike being forced into online progression for a single-player upgrade. feder wrote on Sep 25, 2025: "Why would I need to achieve 'Demon rank' in online fights to unlock a single player mode reward?" That quote captures the complaint that has appeared across multiple threads.

Another player, P4L4D1N, went further on Sep 25, 2025: "Remove this dev please. I hate this game mode, i don't want to grind it, i despise it, i bought the game cause it's a beat them all, i despise auto battler, also it's buggy." The same post described an enemy getting stuck in a wall or ceiling. Other players simply want a faster way, but the available sources do not include an official shortcut.

The frustration is compounded by the fact that online rank details are not clearly explained in-game. Several players described learning the Snake to Wolf to Tiger to Demon order through community replies rather than from a tutorial. That makes the stick it to the stickman demon rank feel more obscure than a standard single-player unlock.

## FAQ

### How do I get the stick it to the stickman demon rank?

Play online Cage Fight and progress from Snake to Wolf, Tiger, then Demon. Players report that ranks come after defeating six sets of other player characters, but the exact win count per rank is not listed in the saved sources.

### Is the stick it to the stickman demon rank required for any single-player unlock?

Yes. Kenari wrote on Oct 1, 2025 that you need at least rank 4, Demon, to unlock a class in another center, which matches the Foreign Manufacturing requirement.

### Can I earn the stick it to the stickman demon rank in single-player?

No. Multiple players confirmed that the stick it to the stickman demon rank is earned in online matches only, not in single-player.

### What should I do if online Cage Fight disconnects or black-screens?

There is no official solution in the saved sources. Player reports from late September and early October 2025 describe connection failures, black screens after rounds, and arena glitches, but other players were still able to play online on Oct 1. Check the Steam Community discussions for updates and consider restarting the mode if you hit a black screen.
