## The Fulfillment Center Secret Character

The fulfillment center secret character is the unlock the guide is named after. The author originally called it the "new secret character" and later renamed it once the character had been out for a while, which tells you this is one of the newer Stick It to the Stickman unlockables added during the post-launch updates.

The route has three stages: get a camera, photograph every web, then arrest the criminals. The guide stresses that the whole chain is a spoiler-free walkthrough, meaning it tells you where to go without revealing what happens at the end of the chain, and it keeps the character's identity hidden until you complete the final delivery.

Players hunting Stick It to the Stickman unlockables often ask whether the camera only drops in specific districts. The guide does not say, so treat every present district as fair game until your inventory proves otherwise.

## Step 1: Getting a Camera

The first step is simple in theory and slow in practice: go around the city opening presents until you get a camera. The guide is honest that this can take a while sometimes, because presents give out random items and the camera is just one of the possible drops.

Community reports add the obvious advice: open every present you pass during normal runs rather than grinding for the camera in one sitting. The drop is random, so a dedicated grind can burn a full evening, while passively collecting during regular play spreads the wait over runs you were going to do anyway.

If you have been opening presents for several runs without a camera, the guide offers no trick: it is a random drop, and persistence is the entire strategy.

## Step 2: Photographing the Webs

Once the camera is in your inventory, go to all of the webs around the map and use the camera to take a picture of each one. The guide gives three locations: up and left of the fulfillment center, far left of the finance tower, and down and right of the evaluation center.

The guide warns that taking the photos can be buggy sometimes. If a photo does not register, the common fix is to reposition and try again, because the detection is picky about where the stickman stands when the picture is taken, and a slightly different angle usually resolves it.

| Web location | Position on the map |
| --- | --- |

| Second web | Far left of the finance tower |
| Third web | Down and right of the evaluation center |
| Photo issues | Can be buggy, reposition and retry |

## Step 3: Arresting the Criminals

If you did it correctly, a phone icon will appear. That is your confirmation the photos registered, and it triggers the next stage: a lasso will appear, so pick it up.

Now the game will mark 3 criminals on the map. Use the lasso to bring each one to the door of the police station, and once all three are delivered, you should unlock the new character. This is the payoff stage of the whole chain, and the guide keeps the identity of the character hidden so the reveal stays fun.

The lasso only spawns after the phone icon appears, so if the lasso is missing, the photos are the place to look first.

| Confirmation | Wait for the phone icon |
| Tool | Pick up the lasso |
| Targets | 3 marked criminals |
| Delivery | Bring them to the police station door |
