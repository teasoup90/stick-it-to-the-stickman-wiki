## The Saitama Route

Getting Saitama has not changed at all according to the guide, and for anyone who is not a veteran the steps are: unlock the gym, find the gym room in a run, do 100 push ups, do 100 situps, do 100 squats, then die or win the run. After that, Saitama is unlocked.

The workout totals are the gate, not the difficulty: the guide lists the counts as plain requirements, and finishing the run in either direction is what banks the unlock.

One comment in the guide's thread jokes that they did 100 push ups and the game started falling apart with black screens, which is a reminder that the unlock count is genuinely high and the game can get silly about it.

| Saitama step | Instruction |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Unlock the gym |
| 2 | Find the gym room in a run |
| 3 | 100 push ups, 100 situps, 100 squats |
| 4 | Die or win the run |

## The Dark Knight Route

The dark knight is the newest secret character in the guide, and the route is: unlock the night shift, go around the map and break all 5 of the CEO cardboard cutouts, pick up the radio, kill all the CEO crime bosses that spawn around the map, then go into the night shift menu.

This is the route that sounds most like a black market operation in spirit, with cardboard cutouts, a radio, and crime bosses, but the game frames it as a night shift mission and nothing in the saved text calls it a market.

Breaking all five cutouts is the checkpoint in the middle of the chain: the radio only matters once the cutouts are down, and the crime bosses are the cleanup phase before the night shift menu completes the unlock.

| Dark Knight step | Instruction |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Unlock the night shift |
| 2 | Break all 5 CEO cardboard cutouts |
| 3 | Pick up the radio |
| 4 | Kill all CEO crime bosses |
| 5 | Go into the night shift menu |

## The Spider-Man Route

They added Spider-Man in the newest update, and the guide warns he is really buggy right now. The route is: find a camera, take pictures of spider webs all around the map, pick up the phone, catch all the criminals, then go to the Fulfillment Center.

The community comments around this route are mostly meme noise, including an ASCII tank and one player announcing they are going to try and get batman. The useful signal is the bug warning from the author, which matches the separate fulfillment center guide's note that the photo step can be buggy.

If the route stalls, the camera and phone steps are the likely culprits, because both guides flag them, while the delivery steps at the Fulfillment Center are reported as the final trigger with no similar warnings attached.
